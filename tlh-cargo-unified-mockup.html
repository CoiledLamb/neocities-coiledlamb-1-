tlh — unified cargo widget mockup v0.0.9.6.9.30-wip

current HUD splits cargo slots (volume) and weight segs (mass) into two unrelated grids. this mock keeps the palette + 2-row geometry and tries 3 ways to fuse them into one "bag" object. three load scenarios shown per variant:
— light: 6/6 slots, 5/10 kg (teal)
— mid: 6/6 slots, 7/10 kg after a heavy-m swap (purple)
— near-max: 6/6 slots, 9/10 kg (pink)

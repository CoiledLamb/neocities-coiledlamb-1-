What you're seeing: the route-map panel expanded into a 2D plane. The dark outer area is off-map void; the rounded-square interior is the playable landmass. The ring is drawn as a solid line connecting the 6 existing nodes — it reads as "a road on a plane" rather than abstract arrows. Nodes keep their existing topology (α, ?, β, γ, η, ·); final rim layout waits for a later world-map regen pass.

Courier position = dot moving along the ring road (interpolated from current edge + dotT). No trail yet — trail design lands with shortcut-travel (v0.0.9.3) since that's when non-ring paths become a thing.

Storm (toggle): demonstrates storm renderer generalized to free 2D motion — a translucent blob drifting across the plane rather than clinging to edge line-paths. Generalizes cleanly from the existing spatial storm data (x/y position, intensity).

Shelter-emergence polish (trigger button): typewriter reveal of the confirmed settlement name in the side panel when a node crosses stage-2 → stage-3. Swaps the italic "unconfirmed" placeholder for the real name, character-by-character.

Not in .2: ASCII road glyphs (later structures patch), real terrain types (.5), click-across-ring shortcut paths (.3), new NPC corners (.7), rim-layout redesign (later regen). Side-view play area untouched.

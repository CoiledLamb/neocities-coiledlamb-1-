What you're seeing: the .tlh-viewport play area — side-view strip where the courier @ lives. Backdrop now ramps with the day/night phase (toggle off to compare to static). Sun + moon are the same radius (real-scale — sets up future eclipses). Stars have calmer flicker, ~40% steady.

Arc model: sun travels 0→1500 (left edge → peak → right edge), moon travels 1500→3000 same shape. Below its arc window the body is below the strip's lower bound → invisible.

Layered gradient: cool vertical base (night → day, with b-blue-hour mix at dawn/dusk transitions) always covers the full strip. On top, during sunrise/sunset, a radial warm glow anchored at the sun's horizon x — orange hottest at the sun → pink halo → magenta fringe → transparent. The side of the sky opposite the sun stays cool. Glow follows sun: left at sunrise, right at sunset.

Warm window peaks while the sun is visibly low (tick ~80 sunrise, ~1435 sunset). Sunrise + sunset curves are separate — can be tuned independently.
